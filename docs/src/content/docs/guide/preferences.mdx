**Ajustes** (Settings), in the header, opens its own screen. It starts on a page with a card for each section (its icon, its name and what it holds), in five blocks: **General** (Uso, Apariencia and the desktop app), **Mirar** (Watching: playback, audio and subtitles, the agent), **Videos** (adding and downloading, exporting, glossary), **Bibliotecas y acceso** (Libraries and access) and **Equipo** (Computer: performance, storage, trash). The search box at the top also finds the options inside each section (for example «subtítulos» leads to Audio y subtítulos). **Ajustes**, above each section's title (or the header's Ajustes button), goes back to that page. On the left, the same sections by block; on the right, the page for the chosen one, with its options grouped and a description of each. Yes/no options are switches; the rest are buttons to choose an option. Changes apply instantly, with no save button. Tapping **Ajustes** again takes you back to the video or the library.

They are saved on the server (`data/prefs.json`) and apply to all tabs and devices. Some belong to each library and are saved in it (see [Libraries](../libraries/#what-each-one-stores)). Only the owner can change them.

The full table, with each option and its default value, is in the [preferences reference](../../reference/preferences/).

## Use

**Uso** (Use), the first page: **Idioma** (Language), one choice for the interface, the translation and the voice, the agent and the summaries: Español, English, Português or **Del sistema** (the system's, the default; installations from before there were languages stay in Spanish). Changing it doesn't reload the page. **Traducir a** (Translate to) separates the language videos are translated and dubbed into: by default **El idioma de la app** (the app's language), or one of the three; what was already translated into other languages is kept and comes back if you choose them again. How it works: [Languages](../../how-it-works/languages/).

**Usar Translay para** (Use Translay for) **Traducir y doblar** (Translate and dub, the default) or **Transcribir y resumir** (Transcribe and summarize). The second is a mode for recording and saving videos to use their transcript and summaries: nothing about translation or voice appears (the audio switch, the split view, Spanish subtitles and voice, the Glossary page, «Traducir y doblar» in menus and the Add panel, and the translated exports), «Audio y subtítulos» becomes «Subtítulos», and the server doesn't load the translator or the voice. Each video is transcribed in the language it's spoken in (a video in English stays in English; its summary is in the app's language). What was already translated is kept, and what you add in this mode stays as «only transcribe», so going back to «Traducir y doblar» doesn't start translating everything at once.
## Libraries

**Bibliotecas** (Libraries): your libraries as cards, with their folder: open them, edit them, change their pattern, remove them from the list or delete them. **Abrir carpeta** (Open folder) adds a folder that is already a library. All of this is explained in [Libraries](../libraries/).

## Appearance

**Apariencia** (Appearance). The **Modo** (Mode): light, dark or whatever your system uses. It belongs to each library, so you can have one light and another dark. **Color de énfasis** (Accent color) is the color of buttons, progress and playback: the theme's or one of seven. With **Color de cada biblioteca** (Color of each library), they take the open library's color instead.

## Playback

**Reproducción** (Playback). What happens when a video opens and when it ends: whether it starts on its own when ready, whether it continues with the next one in **Siguientes** (Up next), and whether it pauses when it reaches a part that doesn't have a Spanish voice yet. Also the default view (expanded or split), when the "Listo para reproducir" (Ready to play) indicator is shown, and what to do with a link you add while watching another video. How each one behaves: [Player](../player/#autoplay) and [Add videos](../import/#while-you-watch-a-video).

## Audio and subtitles

**Audio y subtítulos** (Audio and subtitles). Which audio each video starts with (Spanish or original; you switch later over the video), how loud the original stays under the Spanish voice (silent, low or medium), and which subtitles are shown: Spanish at the bottom, the original's at the top (**Subtítulos del original**, Original subtitles), or none. The original's level is also used when you [export](../export/). **Texto del video en español** (Video text in Spanish) translates what is written in the video and puts it on top in the same place ([how](../../how-it-works/screen-text/)).

## Add and download

**Agregar y descargar** (Add and download). What is done with what you add (download and translate, or download only), which language is only transcribed when detected (**Solo transcribir videos en**, Only transcribe videos in: Spanish by default, English, Portuguese or none), what is downloaded (video and audio, or audio only), in what order downloads run (the queue's or random), browser notifications, whether channels appear in the library sidebar, and the YouTube session used to download (none, the connected account, a browser or a `cookies.txt` file), with the buttons to connect your account or import a session (see [YouTube](../youtube/#youtube-session)). **Al agregar** (On add) and **Qué descargar** (What to download) belong to each library. Details in [Add videos](../import/#download-only-or-translate), [Add videos › «Grabar» and «Agregar»](../import/#grabar-and-agregar), [Add videos › Language](../import/#language-and-transcribe-only) and [Queue](../queue/#downloads).

## Agent

**Agente** (Agent): its name, its **Avatar** (the star you build: shape, eyes, mouth, cheeks, accessory, or **Al azar**), its color (the agent's or the accent color), the tone of its answers, its model and the device it runs on. See [Transcript › Agent](../transcript-and-people/#agent) and [Agent](../../how-it-works/agent/).

## Summary

**Resumen** (Summary), in **Agente**. When each video is summarized (**Resumir**: **Al procesar cada video**, When each video is processed, the default, as soon as the queue finishes it, or **Manualmente**, Manually, from «Generar el resumen» among the agent's suggestions). It uses the agent's model. See [Transcript › Summary](../transcript-and-people/#summary).

## Export

**Exportar** (Export), only on the computer where Translay runs. **Al exportar** (On export): **Preguntar** (Ask, the default) opens the Windows dialog in the last folder you used; with a fixed folder, everything is saved there without asking. From other devices the browser downloads it. See [Export › Where it's saved](../export/#where-its-saved).

## Glossary

**Glosario** (Glossary). Terms that are not translated or that are always translated the same way, specific to the open library. See [Glossary and search](../glossary-and-search/).

## Access and API

**Acceso y API** (Access and API). Who gets in from other devices: the owner's password, local network access, each library's access (private, password-protected or public), API keys and webhooks. See [Access](../../integrations/access/), [API](../../integrations/api/) and [Webhooks](../../integrations/webhooks/).

## Performance

**Rendimiento** (Performance). Your machine, the model profile (automatic or the one you choose), VRAM, RAM and CPU limits, how long a video takes and warnings about why something is slow. See [Performance](../performance/).

## Storage

**Almacenamiento** (Storage). How much each thing takes and what you can free: downloads never translated, the largest ones one by one, models you don't use, the trash, loose files and the installer cache. See [Storage](../storage/).

## Trash

**Papelera** (Trash). What was deleted in the last 30 days, to restore it or delete it forever. See [Trash](../trash/).

## Desktop app

**App de escritorio** (Desktop app). Only in the Windows app: what the window does when you close it (quit or stay in the tray), the Main library folder, and logs. See [Desktop app](../desktop-app/).
