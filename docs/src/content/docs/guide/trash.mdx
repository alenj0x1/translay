Nothing is deleted right away. Deleting a video, emptying a library or deleting it moves everything to the **papelera** (trash), where it stays for 30 days.

## What goes to the trash

- **Videos** (**Eliminar** (Delete) on the selection or **Eliminar video** (Delete video) in the context menu): their data, the translation, the voices, what was downloaded and which collections they were in.
- **An emptied library** (**Vaciar…** (Empty…) in the library's "⋯" menu): all its videos and its collections.
- **A deleted library** (**Eliminar**): the whole library, with its settings and its own trash.

Files are moved, not copied: it is instant and takes no extra space.

## Undo

When you delete, a notice appears with **Deshacer** (Undo): one click and everything is back as it was.

## Restore or delete forever

In **Preferencias › Papelera** (Preferences › Trash) is the list, with when it was deleted and how many days it has left. Each entry has **Restaurar** (Restore; it returns to its library, with its collections) and **Borrar para siempre** (Delete forever). **Vaciar la papelera** (Empty trash) deletes everything forever.

On startup, Translay deletes whatever is older than 30 days.

## Confirm by typing the name

**Vaciar…** and **Eliminar** on a library ask you to type its name to confirm, so it can't be triggered by a stray click. **Vaciar…** is in each library's "⋯" menu (Preferences › Bibliotecas or the **Bibliotecas** screen), no longer in the **Todos** header.

<Aside>
Each library keeps its trash in `<library folder>/papelera/`; deleted libraries go to the Main library's (`data/papelera/`). An entry is a folder with `entry.json` (what it was and what it contained) and `files/` (the files, with their path).
</Aside>
